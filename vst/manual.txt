= START
# PotentialWarp: bend time on the beat
A tempo-locked mangler. Tap one of 16 patterns and whatever runs through it stutters, drops to half speed, runs down like a stopped tape, spins back, plays backwards or gates in time with your beat.

- Add it as an insert effect on a track, program, pad or bus. It is not in the instrument list.
- Press Play on the MPC, then tap a pattern.
- Try Half Time or Tape Stop on a drum bus, Stutter or Roll Up on a break, Reverse on a vocal chop.
- The strip at the top shows the pattern step by step, with a light under the step that is playing.

The full PotentialSuite manual: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/potential-plugins

= CONTROLS
# Controls
- PATTERNS: 16 one-tap pads, also in the MPC's PRESET menu.
- LENGTH: how long the 16 steps last. 1/2 BAR, 1 BAR, 2 BARS or 4 BARS.
- SWING: 50 % (straight) to 75 %, MPC style.
- FADE: TIGHT (short, punchy jumps) or SOFT (smoother).
- WARP: ON or OFF. Automate it to drop the effect in and out.
- PLAY: ALWAYS runs with the song. ONE SHOT plays a pattern once from the next 16th. HOLD plays only while you hold a pad. Pads A01 to A16 play patterns 1 to 16.
- STOP FILTER: a low-pass that closes as a Tape Stop runs down and opens as a Tape Start spins up.
- MIX: blend with the original sound. OUTPUT: the level after the effect.

= PATTERNS
# Patterns and moves
Half Time, Tape Stop, Stop Start, Stutter, Roll Up, Beat Repeat, Reverse, Spinback, Gate 16ths, Gate 8ths, Chop, Half End, Slow Mo, Glitch, Wobble and Mangle.

Each of the 16 steps does one move:
- Straight: the sound as it is. Silent: gates the sound out.
- 1/2 or 1/4 speed: drops an octave or two and drags behind the beat.
- Tape stop or start: slows to a stop, or spins up from silence.
- Repeat: stutters a slice, from an 8th down to a 64th roll, with triplets.
- Backwards or Spinback: the last moment in reverse, or a fast spinback.

= EDIT
# EDIT and CHAIN pages
- EDIT: tap a step, then tap what it does. Steps next to each other with the same move join into one longer move.
- ROLL rolls a brand-new pattern for the pad. MILD, MEDIUM or WILD sets how far it goes.
- RESET PAD puts the pad's own pattern back. An edited pad shows EDITED after its name.
- Every pad's pattern is saved with your project.

- CHAIN: tap a slot (1 to 8), then the pattern for it, and turn CHAIN on.
- The slots play in turn, one LENGTH each, and start again after the last one or at the first END.
- A chain like Half Time, Half Time, Stutter, Tape Stop changes over a phrase by itself.

= SUITE
# With the other plugins
- Put Warp last on the inserts, after Punch, Grit, Bleed and Patina, so it bends the finished sound.
- After the Break Bus chain it gives half time and tape stops on a whole dusty drum bus.
- Grit's ECHO and CHOP are also locked to the tempo. Use one time-based effect at a time for a clean result, or stack them for chaos.
- For a live feel, map WARP or PATTERN to a Q-Link and record the moves as automation.

= HELP
# Good to know
- It follows the song position. Patterns line up with your bars while the sequence plays. When it is stopped, it keeps its own time at the project tempo.
- 4/4 time: one bar is four beats.
- It can only use what it has already heard: backwards plays the moment just before, in reverse.
- HOLD needs pads or keys. The touchscreen does not tell a plugin when your finger lifts.
- Q-Links: PATTERN, LENGTH, MIX, OUTPUT, WARP, FADE, SWING and PLAY.
- Looks: Print Plate, Night Rack, Colour Block, Vintage or Hi-Fi, on the SETTINGS page.
